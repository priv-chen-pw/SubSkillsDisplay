# Changelog

## 1.1.0

* The tab is now **Extra Skills**, with a line saying what it shows. It is always there, and lists every unlocked extra skill whatever the loadout (no Only current loadout setting any more).
* Skills are in lines per type (Primary, Secondary...), like the Loadout tab, without its selection: Harrowing Chains is in the Secondary line. Hover one for its description.
* Hovering a skill also explains its keywords, including those its description names (Stunning, Freezing...).
* Captain's beacons, Railgunner's scoped and charged shots and Active Reload, and Operator's drones are back.
* Pause menu Abilities: lines per type, each of your skills followed by what it turns into.
* README: skill icons.

## 1.0.0

* Extra tab in character select, after Loadout: Acolyte's Anima Harvester, Litany and Blade Dash, Void Fiend's corrupted skills, CHEF's boosted skills, Drifter's bag skills and final slam.
* Abilities in the pause menu during a run: your skills and their extra skills.
* Modded survivors: skills their mod swaps in that no loadout offers.
* Skills from locked loadout choices aren't shown.
